Why Endive

Grown twice. Once in the field, once in the dark.

Belgian endive was found by accident around 1830, when a farmer's stored chicory roots sprouted in a dark cellar. It is grown twice, once in the field and once in the dark, and it thrives where little else does.

That is roughly how we like to work.
