01 · Logo

One logo, a few versions.

The emblem and the words “Endive Limited” always travel together in the same arrangement. Pick the version that suits the background; never build your own.

02 · Emblem

Four bands, one open centre.

The emblem is four bands woven around an open centre: things coming together to fix something small. It can stand alone where the name is already clear, such as app icons, avatars and favicons.

  • On screen it may animate once on arrival, each ring turning into place. Never loop it.
  • Below 32 px the fine stripes merge. That is expected; use the favicon files, which are tuned for small sizes.
  • Keep it Endive Green on light backgrounds, white on green, and Mint on dark.
03 · Clear space & size

Give it room to breathe.

Keep a clear margin around the logo at least the height of the “E” in Endive on every side. Nothing else (text, edges, other logos) goes inside it.

x = the cap height of the “E” in Endive (about a third of the logo’s height).

Logo minimum: 120 px / 30 mm wide
Emblem minimum: 24 px / 6 mm

Please don’t.

The logo files are final artwork. If a version you need is missing, ask at hello@endive.ie rather than editing one.
